What to feed her

Please be careful about what you feed her!

Good to give

  • Kitten wet food or dry kitten food
  • Plain cooked chicken or fish (no bones, salt or spices)
  • Fresh, clean water
  • If you are at Calcutta Rowing Club, please ask a waiter to give you the dry kitten food that has been donated there.

Please avoid

  • Fried food
  • Milk, as most cats can’t digest it
  • Onion, garlic, chocolate, grapes and raisins
  • Spicy, oily or salty leftovers
  • Cooked bones
